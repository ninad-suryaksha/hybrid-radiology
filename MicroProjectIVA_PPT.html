Making the invisible visible.

Global equalisation stretches one histogram over the whole film, so local detail is lost.

CLAHE equalises 64 tiles separately, recovering contrast where it is weakest.

The clip limit caps each tile’s histogram, so noise is not amplified into fake edges.

→ Earlier detection
Contrast that adapts per tile makes faint basal and apical opacities visible before they become obvious.

Dice doesn’t let you cheat.

Dice = 2|P ∩ G| / (|P| + |G|)

LDice = 1 − Dice

L = LBCE + LDice

Worked example: 100 predicted pixels, 120 true, 90 in common. Dice = 180/220 ≈ 0.82; loss ≈ 0.18. BCE gives smooth early gradients and punishes confident errors. Dice scores region agreement, so a “background everywhere” predictor is punished even when the target is a minority of pixels.

Five ways to ask why.

1 Probability heatmap

JET-style map, α = 0.5 over the CLAHE image. Shows the model’s own output, not a gradient attribution.

2 Seg-Grad-CAM

Decoder-stage activation map, gradient-weighted, ReLU’d, normalised to [0, 1].

3 Attention rollout

8×8 token importance. A′ = norm(0.5A + 0.5I), multiplied across layers.

4 Uncertainty

Entropy H(p) and TTA standard deviation, T = 6: flip on/off × −5°, 0°, +5°.

5 Calibration

10-bin reliability diagram, dashed diagonal, ECE.

→ Earlier detection
The uncertainty map marks pixels whose label flips under a trivial perturbation. Those are exactly the borderline opacities a fatigued reader skims past.

From pixels to regions.

A predicted box is a true positive if its IoU with an unmatched ground-truth box is at least 0.5, matched greedily from the highest-scoring prediction. Precision, recall and F1 are computed at box level.

This is not a trained detector and not COCO-style average precision. Boxes are derived from the segmentation mask.

≠ Existing tools

Most tools: segment without localising for the report, or box without a shape. This: contours and boxes from one forward pass.

Knowing when to doubt.

ECE = Σb (|Bb| / n) · |acc(Bb) − conf(Bb)|

A model that says 0.8 should be right about 80% of the time it says 0.8.

Where it is over-confident, the clinician learns to discount it.

ECE weights each bin’s gap between confidence and frequency by its size.

Fig. 7 — Reliability diagram (schematic). ECE = 0 is perfect calibration.

What this is not.

The target is ANATOMY, not disease.

Trained on lung-field masks, the outputs mark where the model believes lung lies. They are not diagnostic.

Detection is DERIVED, not learned.

Boxes come from connected components of the predicted mask, not from a trained detector.

Explanations are NOT clinically validated.

Seg-Grad-CAM and attention rollout have not been compared with radiologists’ judgements.

The split is INTERNAL.

A random 15% hold-out from two institutions; no external test set.

The data is SMALL.

Roughly 700 pairs; disease-mask datasets are smaller still.

The input size is FIXED.

256×256, set by the bottleneck’s positional embedding.

Research prototype. Not a medical device. Not for clinical use.
